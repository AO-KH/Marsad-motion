## The grid

Both films run on the client's funk track, lightbeatsmusic "Joyful Rhythm Walk Funk" (`fit/lightbeats-joyful-rhythm-walk-funk.mp3`, 115 BPM).
- Film beat k is at `k × 0.5217` s: `M.B(k)`.
- An 8th is `M.S8` (0.26 s), and a 16th is `M.S16` (0.13 s).
- The track's sections, in song beats: `references/audio.md`.
- **The landmarks a film uses:**
  - the groove's first beat: the turn;
  - groove B: a lift for a product proof;
  - the one-bar break (song k60–63): a held line;
  - the hit after it (song k64): the end.

## Beat maps for new films (the funk track)

Each map is an `edit` for `film.json` with its landmarks in film beats. Plan shots of 4–10 beats between the landmarks.

| Length | `edit` (song beats) | Beats | Landmarks (film beats) | Shot plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **30 s** (the ontology film; the starter) | `[[8,48],[56,73]]` | 57 | groove k8 · break k44–47 · hit k48 | hook k0–8 · turn k8 · 3–4 shots k8–40 · a held line k40–48 · end k48–57 |
| **44 s** (derived, not yet rendered) | `[[8,48],[64,80],[48,64],[64,76]]` | 84 | groove k8 · groove B k40 · break k68–71 · hit k72 | hook k0–8 · turn k8 · shots k8–40 · a proof lifted by groove B k40 · shots to k66 · a held line k66–72 · end k72–84 |
| **48 s** (the 48 s film) | `[[0,48],[64,80],[48,64],[64,76]]` | 92 | groove k16 · groove B k48 · break k76–79 · hit k80 | hook and problem k0–16 · turn k16 · shots k24–48 · proof k48 · shots to k74 · the breath k74–80 · end k80–92 |
| **60 s** (tested in the 48 s film's earlier cut, commit add0cec) | `[[0,16],[8,16],[16,48],[64,96],[48,64],[64,76]]` | 116 | groove k24 · groove B k56 · break k100–103 · hit k104 | hook and problem k0–24 · turn k24 · shots to k56 · proof k56 · shots to k98 · the breath k98–104 · end k104–116 |

- **Start and join sections on bar lines** (song beats divisible by 4), so film beat 0 is a downbeat and the grid runs straight through the joins. The joins in these maps have all been heard in a render: 47 → 56 and 47 → 64, 79 → 48, 95 → 48, and the intro's second half repeated (15 → 8). A join at the same place in a 16-beat row (song beats 0, 16, 32 …) is the safest.
- **Map a new length the same way:** the hook in the intro, the turn on a groove's first beat, and the end on the hit after the break (song k63 → k64, the last section `[48 or 56, 64 + 9 to 12]`). Check a new map with a `SUB=1` draft, listening at the joins.
